# Keeping your work

Where Zeitlos keeps your files, how to take them with you on a USB stick, how
to back them up and get them back with **backup**, how **update**
brings the system up to date without touching your work, and how to
keep important data safe for years.

You should already know the desktop, the dock, windows, and how to
open files and folders in **files**, as taught in
[Getting started](course:zeit-start).
