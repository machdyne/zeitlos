# Making things

How to make things with the apps that come with Zeitlos: cards, slides
and shows in `slide`; black-and-white paintings in `draw`; pixel art,
icons and small animations in `pix`; and, last, a deck of cards that
works as a small app of its own.

You should already know your way around the desktop: the mouse and
keys, windows, the dock, files and folders, and F1 for help, as
*Getting started* teaches ([Getting started](course:zeit-start)).
