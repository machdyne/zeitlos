# The FPGA

The chip in your machine that becomes the whole computer: what it is,
the gateware that makes it Zeitlos, and how to build gateware of your
own with `zfpga`, on the machine itself ([docs:zfpga](docs:zfpga)).

You should already know your way around the desktop (*Getting
started*) and have met digital logic, FPGAs and Verilog in *Timeless
Computing*: gates, truth tables, flip-flops, clocks, counters, and how
a design becomes a bitstream. To build designs yourself you need an
ECP5 board with at least 32 MB of memory and its card, such as a
Lakritz or a Mozart.
