# Records and numbers

How to keep numbers and records in `sheet`, the spreadsheet that comes
with Zeitlos ([docs:sheet_app](docs:sheet_app)): cells, formulas,
number formats and charts, and then two things of your own to keep, a
budget and a log.

You should already know how to use the desktop, the mouse and the
keys, windows, the dock, and files and folders, as the course *Getting
started* teaches them. No arithmetic beyond adding, multiplying and
percentages is needed.
