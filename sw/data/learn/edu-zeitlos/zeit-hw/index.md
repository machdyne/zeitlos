# Connecting things

How to connect real things to a Zeitlos computer, and how to talk to
them: the PMOD connectors, the GPIO pins on them, the I2C and SPI buses
and serial ports, the bench where virtual parts can be tried first, and
Machdyne's modules.

You should already know the desktop from *Getting started* (opening
apps from the dock, windows, `term`), and the Electricity and Talking to
the World units of *Timeless Computing*: voltage, current, Ohm's law,
logic levels, pull-ups, working safely, and what UART, SPI and I2C are.
