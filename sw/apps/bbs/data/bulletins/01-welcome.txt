This board runs on Zeitlos. Bulletins are text files in the bulletins
folder of the BBS's data directory, shown in name order; each caller
sees the ones changed since their last call, and all of them from the
menu.

Write them in UTF-8. |14Pipe codes|07 set colours: ||12 is |12bright red|07,
||07 back to grey. ||RV and ||RO turn |RV reverse video |RO on and off, which
every terminal shows -- even one without colour. docs/bbs.md lists them all.
